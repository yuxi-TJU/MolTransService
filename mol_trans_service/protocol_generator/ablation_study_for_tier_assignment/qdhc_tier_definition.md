# QDHC Tier Definitions

- L1: Wide-band-limit transport treatment with energy-independent molecule–electrode coupling terms.
- L2: Self-consistent cluster-based transport treatment with explicit local molecule–electrode contact structure.
- L3: NEGF-based transport treatment with semi-infinite electrodes, capable of treating non-equilibrium transport under finite bias.
- Out-of-Scope: Problems requiring physical effects or methodological treatments not included in the current three-tier framework for coherent charge transport in standard single-molecule junctions, including non-gold electrodes, incoherent or inelastic transport, and spin- or thermoelectric-related treatments.
